The vast majority of pickleball is played by amateurs. The vast majority of pickleball content is about pros. That gap has nothing to do with where the magic happens — many of the best moments in the sport happen at rec clubs every day. It's about who has the camera, and who has the audience. PodPlay Technologies-powered clubs are now generating 1.5M annualized replays, and growing fast. Every PodPlay Pro court is recording every rally. The "who has the camera" problem is solved. This week we shipped Go Viral — one tap that puts a player's replay in front of PodPlay's distribution partners (PodPlay's Best of Pickleball with Kaitlyn Kerr, ClipTHAT with The Dink Pickleball, Top 10 Amateur Pickleball Highlights with The Kitchen Pickleball) and into their club's Go Viral admin dashboard, where any GM can pull it straight into the club's social feed. "Who has the audience" — now solved too. The best amateur pickleball content in the world isn't going to come from the top down. It's going to come from rec courts at clubs all over the country.
